04 / FRAMEWORK REFERENCE

One continuous feedback loop.

01

Operational data

Normalize network, cloud and IoT logs, alerts, traffic and device status.

02

Digital twin

Map assets, topology, service dependencies and current security conditions.

03

Coordinated agents

Connect anomaly detection, threat intelligence, attack prediction and risk analysis.

04

Response evaluation

Simulate candidate actions and compare threat reduction with service disruption.

05

Authorized response

Present recommendations for analyst review, approval and recorded rationale.

06

Outcome monitoring

Check response effectiveness and availability, then refresh the twin’s context.
